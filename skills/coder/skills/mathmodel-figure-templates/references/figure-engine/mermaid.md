# Mermaid engine

Mermaid is the lightweight engine for draft flows, state diagrams, sequence sketches, and Markdown-native documentation. It is useful while agreeing on topology before producing a final TikZ or DrawIO figure.

Use Mermaid as a final paper asset only when the target route accepts its exported SVG/PDF and the rendered typography has passed the same QA as other engines. Do not rely on browser-default Mermaid themes for MCM/CUMCM or journal delivery. Preserve the `.mmd` source and record the renderer version because layout can change across releases.

If formulas, precise anchors, custom panel composition, or stable cross-platform geometry matter, migrate the approved Mermaid topology to TikZ or DrawIO.
