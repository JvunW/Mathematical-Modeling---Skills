# IEEE profile

Target dense but legible engineering communication, commonly read in one- or two-column layouts and often printed in grayscale.

- Size at the intended column width before tuning labels.
- Use the paper's approved font family consistently; keep mathematical symbols compatible with the manuscript.
- Prefer line styles, markers, hatching, and labels in addition to colour.
- Keep axes, units, legends, and callouts compact; avoid legends over data.
- Use vector PDF/EPS/SVG working files as supported by the target venue and embed fonts.
- For architecture diagrams, prioritize signal direction, interfaces, and boundary conditions over decorative Panels.
- Run a grayscale audit and verify that thin lines and small markers survive PDF rendering.

IEEE societies and conferences differ. Check the exact template and author instructions instead of hard-coding one universal point size.
