# DrawIO engine

Use DrawIO for editable technical roadmaps, data-processing flows, indicator hierarchies, decision trees, or concept maps where manual rearrangement is valuable and mathematical typesetting is secondary.

Route execution to `$mathmodel-drawio`. Apply the selected style profile from this skill to its font, palette, border, spacing, and hierarchy decisions.

Do not use DrawIO for numeric curves, statistical distributions, dense weighted networks, or structures whose meaning depends on exact formulas. Export the `.drawio` source and PDF; render a PNG for QA. Verify that connectors terminate at intended shapes and that export has not substituted fonts.
